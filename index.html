PBP Financials

Overview

Bring in this month's bookings

  1. Pick the month at the top right.
  2. Export your reservations from Hospitable and drop the file below. The .zip works exactly as it downloads.
  3. Check the column choices and match any property names the app didn't recognize.
  4. Apply. Gross revenue and commission fill in, and every property gets a draft invoice on the Invoices page.

The file is read in your browser and isn't uploaded anywhere.
